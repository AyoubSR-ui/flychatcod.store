Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 26, 2026

FlyChat COD ("we", "us", or "our") operates the FlyChat COD platform (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Customer data: Names, phone numbers, addresses, and order information provided by customers through messaging channels (WhatsApp, Instagram DMs).
  • Store owner data: Email addresses, store names, and business information provided during registration.
  • Messaging data: Conversation content between customers and store owners through connected channels.
  • Usage data: Information on how the Service is accessed and used.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our Service
  • To process and manage customer orders
  • To enable AI-powered customer support
  • To notify you about changes to our Service
  • To provide customer support
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues

3. Instagram and Facebook Data

When you connect your Instagram Business account to FlyChat COD, we access your Instagram Direct Messages solely for the purpose of providing customer support and order management. We do not store Instagram messages beyond what is necessary to operate the Service. We comply with Meta's Platform Terms and Instagram's API usage policies.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to third parties. We may share data with:

  • OpenAI: For AI-powered response generation (conversation content only)
  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram): As required to operate messaging integrations
  • Service providers: Hosting and infrastructure providers (Railway)

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

6. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to processing of your data
  • Data portability

8. Data Deletion

To request deletion of your data, please contact us at support@flychatcod.store. We will process your request within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:
